Delete your game account

These instructions apply to Sudoku Fun and Arrow Escape. In the game you want to delete, open Settings, choose Delete your account, review the confirmation, and confirm. Keep the app open and connected to the internet until it reports completion. The request cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the game, email support@redalphagames.com with the game name and “account deletion” in the subject. Do not send passwords or sign-in tokens. We may ask for information needed to verify that the account is yours before completing the request.

Once accepted, the game signs you out, clears that game's local data on the device, and the server deletes the game's player profile and Firebase sign-in account. Acceptance means the request was queued; Firebase cleanup may still be completing in the background. If the process is interrupted, reopen the same game with an internet connection to retry cleanup, or contact support if you cannot confirm that the request was accepted. After completion, signing in again—even with the same Google account—creates a new game profile. Your Google account itself is not deleted.

Data removed and retained

For the selected game, we delete the server-side player profile and Firebase sign-in account, and the app clears game data stored locally on that device. Progress stored only on that device cannot be recovered after deletion. While deletion is pending, we retain a limited account-linked security record so that failed cleanup can be retried and an old sign-in token cannot recreate the profile. After Firebase account deletion succeeds, this record is scheduled to expire after 65 minutes; automatic physical removal may take additional time. If cleanup fails, the pending record remains until the failure is resolved.

Account deletion does not immediately erase historical backups, operational logs, or support correspondence. These records follow their applicable retention schedules; records required for legal or security reasons may also be retained. See our Privacy Policy for details. Data held independently by your app store or service providers follows their own policies. Deleting a game account does not delete your Google account.
