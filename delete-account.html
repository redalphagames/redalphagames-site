Delete your Sudoku Fun account

In Sudoku Fun, open Settings, choose Delete your account, and confirm. Keep the app open until it reports completion. The request cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email support@redalphagames.com with the subject “Sudoku Fun account deletion”. Do not send passwords or sign-in tokens. We may ask for information needed to verify that the account is yours before completing the request.

Once your request is accepted, you are signed out and deletion continues in the background. Acceptance does not mean deletion has already finished. If the process is interrupted, reopen Sudoku Fun with an internet connection to retry local cleanup. If you see an error or cannot confirm that your request was accepted, contact support. Once deletion is complete, signing in again—even with the same Google account—starts a new Sudoku Fun profile. Your previous progress cannot be restored. Your Google account itself is not deleted.

Data removed and retained

We delete your Sudoku Fun account, associated player profile and saved game progress, your Sudoku Fun sign-in record, and game data stored on your device. While deletion is pending, we retain a limited account-linked deletion record so that failures can be retried and old requests cannot recreate your profile. After account deletion succeeds, this security record is scheduled to expire after 65 minutes; automatic physical removal can take additional time. If deletion fails, the pending record remains until the failure is resolved.

Account deletion does not immediately erase historical backups, operational logs, or support correspondence. These records follow their retention schedules, and records required for legal or security reasons may also be retained. See our Privacy Policy for retention information. Data held independently by your app store or advertising providers follows their own policies.
